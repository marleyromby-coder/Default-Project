# Sign up your business

Join FixMatch for free. Customers nearby compare tradespeople and garages on price, distance and reviews from FixMatch users – and call you directly.

- **Free.** No fees per customer or per call.
- **You decide.** We only show what you write here, and you can always have it corrected or removed.
- **Nobody can buy their way to the top.** The order is based on price, distance and reviews from FixMatch users, and each user chooses what matters most. The order cannot be bought. Until you have reviews, you count as 5 neutral reviews of 4.0.
- **Reviews.** Only users who contacted you through FixMatch can review you – once each. We check that they tapped Call or sent your business a message in FixMatch. We cannot see whether the call was answered or whether the job was done.
- **Get verified.** Tick the box and we check your company number, owner and licence, and you get a tick next to your name in the app. That is free too.
