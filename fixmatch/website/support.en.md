# Support

## Frequently asked questions

### Does FixMatch cost anything?

No. The app is free. You only pay the business for the work, and you agree the price directly with them.

### Do I need an account?

You can look at and compare everything without one. The first time you call or message a business through FixMatch, you sign in with Apple and give your name and phone number, so the business knows who it is talking to. Sign in with Apple is the only way to log in. You can delete it under *Profile → Delete account*. Afterwards you can also remove FixMatch from your Apple Account under *Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → FixMatch*.

### What does the tick next to the name mean?

That the business is verified: FixMatch has checked its company number, the owner and the licence (for electrical, plumbing and drainage work), and sometimes its insurance and guarantee scheme too. The badge lasts a year and does not give a better position. It is not a guarantee of the quality of the work.

### How does Quote check work?

You choose the job and type in the price from your quote. FixMatch compares it with the prices nearby businesses have listed themselves and says low, normal or high. It is a guide, and what you type stays on your phone.

### How do you decide the order?

By price, distance and reviews from FixMatch users – you choose what matters most under *Profile → Your priorities*. A business with no reviews counts as 5 neutral reviews of 4.0. The order cannot be bought. Paid ads are always labelled "Ad" and never change the order. The exact description is under *More → How we rank* in the app.

### Who can write reviews?

Users who contacted the business through FixMatch – once each. We check that you tapped Call or sent the business a message in FixMatch. We cannot see whether the call was answered or whether the job was done.

### Why are there no businesses where I live?

FixMatch is only in Denmark for now. We open city by city and only show businesses that have agreed to be listed. Write to us if you would like FixMatch where you live.

### How do I change the language?

FixMatch follows your phone's language. On iPhone, choose it under *Settings → FixMatch → Language*.

### Do you use my location?

Only on your phone, to work out distances, and only if you allow it. It is not sent to us. Read more in the [privacy policy](en/privacy/).

### Is something about a business wrong?

Tap *Report an error or unlawful content* at the bottom of the business page, or write to {{email}}.

### How do I report a review or a group?

Tap *Report* next to the review or at the bottom of the group, and choose why. It is hidden on your phone at once. We look at every report within 24 hours on weekdays and remove offensive content.

### I run a business. How do I get listed?

It is free. Sign up in the app under *More → Sign up your business* or on the website. You approve your details and prices and give your consent when you sign up. You can always have them corrected or removed within 2 working days.
