## Frequently asked questions

### Does FixMatch cost anything?

No. The app is free and needs no login. You only pay the business for the work, and you agree the price directly with them.

### How do you decide the order?

By the FixMatch score, which you can adjust under *More → Your priorities*. No business can pay for a better position. The exact description is under *More → How we rank* in the app.

### Why are there no businesses where I live?

We open city by city and only show businesses that have agreed to be listed. Write to us if you would like FixMatch where you live.

### How do I change the language?

FixMatch follows your phone's language. On iPhone, choose it under *Settings → FixMatch → Language*.

### Do you use my location?

Only on your phone, to work out distances, and only if you allow it. It is not sent to us. Read more in the [privacy policy](en/privacy/).

### Is something about a business wrong?

Tap *Report an error or unlawful content* at the bottom of the business page, or write to {{email}}.

### I run a business. How do I get listed?

It is free. Write to {{email}} and we will send you a consent form where you approve your details and prices. You can always have them corrected or removed within 2 working days.
