## For users

1. **The service.** FixMatch helps you find and compare businesses and contact them directly. FixMatch is free. You can browse and compare without an account; to call a business through FixMatch you create one with your real name and phone number, which the business sees. You can delete the account in the app at any time.
2. **FixMatch is not a party to the agreement.** You make the agreement about the work directly with the business. Your rights as a consumer (such as complaints and remedies) apply against the business.
3. **Information.** Prices, opening hours and other details are provided by the businesses. Prices are a guide and include VAT. We do our best to keep everything correct but cannot guarantee it. Always ask for a written quote.
4. **Reviews.** When FixMatch shows reviews, they come from Trustpilot. FixMatch does not write, edit or select reviews and does not itself check whether they come from real customers.
5. **Ranking.** The order is set by the FixMatch score and your own priorities. The main factors are price and distance – and reviews, when the app shows them. The exact description is under "How we rank" in the app. Payment does not affect the order. Ads are marked "Ad".
6. **Errors and illegal content.** Use "Report an error or unlawful content" on the business's page or write to {{email}}. We handle reports as quickly as we can and tell you what we do (EU Digital Services Act, Article 16).
7. **Liability.** FixMatch is not liable for work done by the businesses or for losses caused by incorrect information, unless mandatory law says otherwise.
8. **Governing law.** Danish law applies. If you live in another country, you keep the mandatory consumer rights you have under the law there, and you can bring a case before the courts of your country.

## For businesses (summary – the full agreement is the consent form)

- The basic listing is free, and there is never a charge per call or per customer.
- The business is responsible for its information being correct and for prices including VAT.
- The main ranking factors are price and distance – and reviews, when the app shows them. Paid extras do not affect the ranking.
- Changes to these terms are announced at least 15 days in advance (EU Platform-to-Business Regulation, Article 3).
- A listing can be removed if its information is incorrect. The business is told why.
- Complaints about FixMatch go to {{email}} and are answered within 10 working days.
