## For users

1. **The service.** FixMatch helps you find and compare businesses and contact them directly. FixMatch is free. You can browse and compare without an account; to call a business through FixMatch you create one with your real name and phone number, which the business sees. You can delete the account in the app at any time.
2. **FixMatch is not a party to the agreement.** You make the agreement about the work directly with the business. Your rights as a consumer (such as complaints and remedies) apply against the business.
3. **Information.** Prices, opening hours and other details are provided by the businesses. Prices are a guide and include VAT. We do our best to keep everything correct but cannot guarantee it. Always ask for a written quote.
4. **Reviews.** Only users who contacted the business through FixMatch can review it – once per business. We check that you tapped Call or Message for the business in FixMatch. We cannot see whether the call was answered or whether the job was done (Danish Marketing Practices Act, section 6 b). We show all reviews, including the bad ones, newest first, and we never change the text. Nobody can pay to have a review removed or moved. We hide reviews that break the rules, and we tell the reviewer why. The rules are on the "About reviews" page in the app. A business can show links to its profiles on other review sites. We do not fetch or check those reviews, and they do not count towards the FixMatch score.
5. **Ranking.** The order is set by the FixMatch score and your own priorities. The main factors are price, distance and reviews from FixMatch users – you choose what matters most. A business with no reviews counts as 5 neutral reviews of 4.0. The exact description is under "How we rank" in the app. The order cannot be bought. Paid ads are always labelled "Ad" and never change the order (Danish Marketing Practices Act, section 6 a).
6. **User content, errors and illegal content.** Reviews, Naborabat groups and photos must not be offensive, threatening, discriminatory or obscene, and must not contain personal information, phone numbers, links or advertising. We have zero tolerance for such content and for users who harass others: we hide the content and can delete the user's account. How to report: tap "Report" on a review or group, use "Report an error or unlawful content" on the business's page, or write to {{email}}. What you report in the app is hidden on your phone at once. We review reports within 24 hours on weekdays. If you write to us, we tell you what we do (EU Digital Services Act, Article 16).
7. **Liability.** FixMatch is not liable for work done by the businesses or for losses caused by incorrect information, unless mandatory law says otherwise.
8. **Governing law.** Danish law applies. If you live in another country, you keep the mandatory consumer rights you have under the law there, and you can bring a case before the courts of your country.

## For businesses (summary – the full agreement is the consent form)

- The basic listing is free, and there is never a charge per call or per customer.
- The business is responsible for its information being correct and for prices including VAT.
- The main ranking factors are price, distance and reviews from FixMatch users. A business with no reviews counts as 5 neutral reviews of 4.0. Paid extras do not affect the ranking.
- Users who contacted the business through FixMatch can review it. FixMatch checks that the user tapped Call or Message for the business in FixMatch, but cannot see whether the call was answered or whether the job was done. The business cannot remove reviews, but it can report a review that breaks the rules.
- The business can give links to its own profiles on other review sites. FixMatch only shows the link – never stars, scores or counts.
- Changes to these terms are announced at least 15 days in advance (EU Platform-to-Business Regulation, Article 3).
- A listing can be removed if its information is incorrect. The business is told why.
- Complaints about FixMatch go to {{email}} and are answered within 10 working days.

> Reviews written in FixMatch became part of the ranking in November 2026, before there were real businesses in the app, so no notice was due. Later changes to the formula must be announced 15 days in advance.
