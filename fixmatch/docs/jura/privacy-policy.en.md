## 1. Data controller

{{publisher}}. Email: {{email}}.

## 2. In short

FixMatch has no ads and no tracking. If you create an account, we store your e-mail, name and phone number in the EU, so the businesses you call through FixMatch know who they are talking to (section 7). Reviews and prices you choose to share are stored too (section 9). Everything else stays on your phone.

## 3. Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location **while it is open** to work out the distance to the businesses. The calculation happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us or anyone else and is only stored on your phone. You can turn access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings and choose a city instead.

## 4. The map (Apple Maps)

The map is shown with Apple Maps (MapKit). To show it, your iPhone loads map images directly from Apple, and Apple handles those requests under its own privacy policy. FixMatch has no access to that data and stores no map data. The businesses' positions on the map are their own addresses, which they have agreed to show.

## 5. "Describe your problem"

The text you write and your budget are only processed on your phone, to find the trade and the businesses that fit. The text is not sent to FixMatch or to the businesses. If you tap "Share the description with a business", you decide who to send it to.

You can add up to three pictures of the problem. The app only uses the camera or your photos when you tap "Take picture" or "Choose from photos" yourself, and only sees the pictures you choose. FixMatch does not store the pictures, and the app forgets them when it closes. If you tap "Send pictures and description" on a business's page, your phone's Messages app opens and you send them to the business's number yourself. FixMatch never receives them.

After a call the app may ask whether anyone answered. If you answer, the answer is stored with your account at Supabase in the EU, so the business's answer rate can be shown. Nobody but FixMatch can see who answered what, and the figure is only shown from 5 answers.

## 6. Data stored on your phone

Your saved businesses, your chosen city, your priorities, your comparison list and the list of businesses you have contacted (under Profile) are stored on your phone so they are there next time. The same goes for everything in "My home" (jobs, amounts, invoices and pictures), your answers to "Did you reach them?" and pictures of quotes in Quote check. They do not leave your phone. You delete them under *More → Reset app data* or by deleting the app.

## 7. Your account

You can browse and compare businesses without an account. The first time you call a business through FixMatch, we ask you to create one, so the business knows who it is talking to.

- **What we store:** your e-mail (or the ID and e-mail Apple gives us when you use "Sign in with Apple" – Apple can hide your real e-mail), your name, your phone number, and which businesses you have called through FixMatch and when.
- **Why:** so you can sign in, and so the business can see the name and number of whoever contacted it through FixMatch. The legal basis is our agreement with you to use FixMatch (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
- **Who sees it:** only the business you called sees your name, phone number and the time. The business sees it on its own page in FixMatch for 90 days after you contacted it, and may only use it to answer you. We never sell the information or use it for advertising.
- **Where:** with our processor Supabase on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
- **How long:** until you delete your account under *Profile → Delete account*. The account, name, phone number and list of calls are then deleted straight away. We delete accounts that have not been used for 24 months.

## 8. When you contact a business

The call itself, websites and directions happen in your phone's own apps or in a browser, and the privacy policy of the business, Apple or the browser then applies. If you have an account, FixMatch records that you called, so the business can see your name and number (section 7).

## 9. Reviews and prices you share

If you contacted a business through FixMatch, you can review it and share what the job cost. This is voluntary.

- **What we store:** your review (stars, text, job and date) and your price (amount, labour if given, job, number of m² or items, month and the first two digits of the business's postcode). Both are linked to your account, so you can only review and share once per business and job, and so we can check that you contacted the business.
- **Who sees it:** everyone in the app sees the review with your initial, never your name. Prices are only shown together as a range, and only once at least 3 people have shared a price for the same job. Nobody can see who shared a price.
- **Why:** so others can choose the right business and see what a job really costs. The legal basis is our agreement with you to use FixMatch (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
- **Where and how long:** with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Prices are shown for 24 months. You change your review or price by doing it again, and everything is deleted when you delete your account.
- **Rules:** do not write names, phone numbers or other personal data in a review. We hide reviews that break the rules. We never change the text.

> Without an account (for example in the demo) reviews and prices stay on the phone.

## 10. Images and data from the internet

The app loads the businesses' own pictures and (if switched on) an updated list of businesses from the internet. As is normal on the internet, the server sees your IP address for these requests. We do not store IP addresses when the business list is loaded.

## 11. Information about businesses

We show information about businesses (such as name, company number, phone number and prices) that the businesses themselves have approved. For sole traders this can be personal data about the owner. The legal basis is the business's consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). If a business signs itself up in the app or on the website, we keep the sign-up with the consent wording and time with Supabase in the EU as proof. The consent can be withdrawn at any time by writing to {{email}}, and we then remove the information within 2 working days. Contact persons' names and email addresses are only used to manage the listing and are kept for as long as the listing exists, and for 12 months after that as proof of the consent.

**Free today.** If a business marks free time on its personal link, we store the days it is free and when it last marked them. They are shown to everyone in the app, and old days are never shown. We only store the code in the link as a hash.

**The Verified badge.** If a business asks for the badge, we also keep its licence number, insurance company and guarantee scheme, and what we checked and when (company number, owner, licence, insurance and guarantee scheme). We only use this to give and renew the badge. The legal basis is the agreement with the business about the badge (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We delete the information when the badge is no longer renewed or the business leaves FixMatch.

> Check the legal basis and retention period for verification with your lawyer (see the GDPR preparation).

## 12. When you write to us

If you write to us, we use your email address and what you write to answer you. We delete the message no later than 12 months after the matter is closed.

## 13. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to data portability. Write to {{email}}. You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, www.datatilsynet.dk. If you live in another country, you can also complain to the authority there, for example the ICO in the United Kingdom.

## 14. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and say so in the app's "What's New".
